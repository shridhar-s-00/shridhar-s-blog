# Reflecting on 24 years of life

## Whats this about
Nothing in particular, but between the smell of flowers that I know I last smelled in Croydon when I was five  and the taste of cheap yoghurt that reminded me of first grade, I can't help but feel nostalgic about my time here. I truly do wish to go back and live those memories once more, and I can't think of many people who don't, but I'd love to share the moments that make me ... me. I'm not fond of pictures, nor do I like to really like social media (I've pretty much deleted most of my digital footprint including Twitter and Instagram) so this is how I interact with you, internet stranger.

This is where normally we start from the beginning, but I think its time we start now, **from the end.**
